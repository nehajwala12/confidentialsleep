Weekly click-outs times 52 times revenue per click-out, plus other revenue, less stand-alone cost, give EBITDA for the twelve months from closing. That is multiplied by the case multiple, reduced by selling cost and the estate, discounted at 15% and multiplied by Comvest's share.

Two readings select the case: whether 2027 partner terms are signed, and whether click-outs hold at 27,000 a week or more. No probabilities or weighted value are shown.

The bridge takes the company's opening cash and collection schedule, revenue for October to March and a transition budget by month. The funding need is the amount that keeps month-end cash at the floor.
